### 🚀 About Me

I am a **Physicist turned Quant** driven by first-principles thinking. My foundation began in **Theoretical Physics & Computational Astrophysics** (General Relativity, Cosmology, and Statistical Mechanics), where I developed rigorous mathematical and stochastic modeling skills. Today, I apply those principles to **Financial Markets**, engineering low-latency trading infrastructure, researching quantitative alpha, and building high-performance systems.

I bridge the gap between **Quantitative Research** (Stochastic Calculus, Bayesian Inference, Numerical Solvers) and **Systems Engineering** (C++, Rust, Swift, Linux/Mach internals), ensuring that sophisticated models execute at microsecond speeds with zero unnecessary overhead.
